# Clean-City-Connect
Clean City Connect – Smart Waste Pickup at Your Doorstep. Choose Your Waste Type, We'll Handle the Rest.
Smart Waste Pickup at Your Doorstep. Choose Your Waste Type, We'll Handle the Rest.Clean City Connect is a smart waste management platform that offers garbage pickup and drop-off services. Users can schedule pickups based on their convenience and select the type of waste — organic, recyclable, or hazardous — for efficient and eco-friendly disposal.
